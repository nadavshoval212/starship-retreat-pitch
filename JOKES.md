# Joke bank — team review

All lines below are fictional pitch copy. Pick a few; don't turn the deck into a wall of punchlines.

## Core business

- “We put the cloud in space so a man buying a taquito in Ohio can feel better than another man buying a taquito in Ohio.”
- “AWS has availability zones. We have loading zones.”
- “Our edge computing is located 400 kilometers away from the edge.”
- “Our customer discovery revealed that truckers wanted cleaner showers. We heard orbital infrastructure.”
- “We are solving the last-mile problem by adding 250 miles vertically.”
- “Our competitors are constrained by physics. We are constrained by the next slide.”

## The loyalty reveal

- “The satellites are a customer acquisition cost.”
- “We're not selling compute. We're selling the right to board a shower before another adult.”
- “Diesel. Gold. Platinum. Divorced.”
- “Blackout dates include Christmas, Thanksgiving, and whenever you actually want to go.”
- “Our points never expire. Their purchasing power dies instantly.”
- “Every purchase earns miles. None of our customers leave the interstate.”
- “We turn diesel into airline miles, airline miles into status, and status into a reason to stay married until the end of the qualification year.”

## Will superhero slide

Art direction: an obviously fantastical comic-book portrait, Will as CAPTAIN SPECIFIC IMPULSE, holding a satellite in one hand and a truck-stop rewards card in the other. Excessive gold shoulder armor, rocket exhaust cape, tiny taquito-shaped spacecraft. Use an appropriate reference photo if likeness is desired.

- Headline: **TECHNICAL RISK: WILL.**
- “Will can get it into orbit. Nobody can explain why it belongs there.”
- “Our entire technical diligence is a photo of Will with his arms crossed.”
- “Will spent years making rockets reusable. We're making that experience regrettable.”
- Small gag label: “Advisor status: within earshot.”

## Starship / Sean / Hugo

- “Starship backs founders undefeated by conventional wisdom. We are undefeated because conventional wisdom refused to get in the ring.”
- “Sean, if this isn't frontier technology, why is there absolutely no civilization anywhere near it?”
- “Hugo's the COO. We assume that means Chief Orbital Officer. We have already put him on the org chart.”
- “Starship is our ideal lead investor. You've already demonstrated the judgment required by inviting us here.”
- “Our fundraising strategy is to say 'sovereign compute' until Sean makes eye contact.”

## LPs and venture theater

- “LP stands for Loyalty Points. You were warned in the acronym.”
- “We can offer liquidity immediately. There's a soda fountain next to pump seven.”
- “Your capital is locked up for ten years. Your shower credits expire Friday.”
- “The TAM is every person who has ever been somewhere and wished they were somewhere else.”
- “We have zero revenue, six advisors, and a defense application. So we believe the round should be competitive.”

## Hotter candidates

- “We looked at a truck-stop bathroom and thought: what this needs is a fucking cap table.”
- “The strongest force in the universe is a middle-aged man being told he's not Platinum anymore.”
- “Our moat is shame. No investor wants to admit they funded a $400 million shower punch card.”
- “We're raising $69 million because apparently saying 'nice' counts as investment committee approval.”
- “This isn't a Ponzi scheme. In a Ponzi scheme, someone eventually gets a return flight.”

## Judge Q&A

**Why space?** “Because if we put it in a warehouse, you'd value us like a warehouse.”

**Who is the customer?** “Initially you. Then whoever you can introduce us to.”

**What if the satellite fails?** “Members retain their status through the remainder of the qualification year.”

**What's proprietary?** “The conversion rate between a shower and a flight to Cleveland.”

**Why truck stops?** “Captive distribution. Literally: federally mandated rest breaks.”

**What is your exit?** “Exit 147.”
