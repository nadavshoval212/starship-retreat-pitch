## Clock

- **0–5 min, all:** pick name, agree on reveal, claim lanes, confirm pitch duration.
- **5–25 min, parallel:** story writer drafts; designer builds slide shell; joke writer supplies strongest lines and specific room references.
- **25–35 min, all:** read the story aloud, choose jokes, lock sequence. Designer integrates.
- **35–45 min:** finish visuals and export. Writer trims for time; joke writer plays hostile judge.
- **45–55 min, all:** two timed rehearsals. Cut anything that needs explanation.
- **55–60 min:** final export, backup PDF, decide who advances slides and who answers questions.

## Suggested onstage division

1. CEO: opens as if this is a real infrastructure company.
2. CTO: explains a completely unnecessary orbital architecture with total conviction.
3. CFO / Chief Loyalty Officer: reveals the points business and makes the ask.
